Score the script 0-10 on each axis. Be critical.

Axes definitions:
- hook (0-10): Does the opening sentence immediately establish a concrete person, date, or event with high curiosity? (Penalize generic greetings or rhetorical questions).
- accuracy (0-10): Are all names, numbers, years, and outcomes faithful to the facts?
- clarity (0-10): Can a total stranger immediately understand the context and what is happening? Is the explanation clear?
- retention (0-10): Does the story build momentum and tension toward a satisfying climax, or does it feel like a random trivia list?
- novelty (0-10): Is the story unique, surprising, and thought-provoking?
- pacing (0-10): Are sentences punchy, rhythmic, and natural to speak aloud?
- visual_potential (0-10): Does the narrative evoke vivid imagery for b-roll and graphics?
- naturalness (0-10): Does it sound like an energetic storyteller talking to a friend?
- policy_risk (0-10): Any copyright, defamatory, hateful, or harmful advice? (0 = perfectly safe, 10 = extreme risk).
